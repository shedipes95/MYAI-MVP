# MyAI - 3-Minute Demo Video Script

## 🎯 **Introduction (15 seconds)**

"Hello! I'm excited to show you MyAI - a revolutionary financial management platform powered by AI, specifically designed for affluent suburban families. Let me walk you through how MyAI transforms complex financial data into actionable insights."

---

## 🔐 **Authentication & Onboarding (30 seconds)**

"First, users sign up with our beautiful, branded login system featuring our signature gradient logo. After authentication, new users go through our personalized onboarding questionnaire."

_[Show login page, then onboarding flow]_

"This captures essential demographic and financial information - housing situation, debt levels, transportation costs, spending patterns, and financial goals. This data enables MyAI to provide highly personalized recommendations tailored to each family's unique situation."

---

## 💬 **AI-Powered Homepage (35 seconds)**

"Here's our main interface - a clean, intuitive chatbot homepage with MyAI branding. Users can interact naturally with our AI assistant using the input field, or select from intelligent prompt suggestions that appear when they focus on the input."

_[Show homepage, click input to show suggestions]_

"Notice these predefined financial questions that help users get started - like 'What are my biggest spending categories?' or 'How can I optimize my monthly budget?' These guide users to ask the right questions about their finances."

_[Show some of the predefined questions]_

"The AI understands context about uploaded financial data and provides real, actionable answers - not generic responses. This creates a conversational experience around personal finance management."

---

## 📊 **Transaction Processing & Insights (35 seconds)**

"Now for the core functionality - intelligent transaction processing. Users simply upload their bank transaction CSV files, and MyAI automatically processes them through our FastAPI backend."

_[Navigate to Ingest page, upload CSV]_

"Watch as MyAI categorizes transactions, calculates spending patterns, and presents beautiful visualizations. We get key performance indicators with traffic-light status indicators - total spend, top spending category, and budget status with green, amber, or red alerts."

_[Show KPI cards and pie chart]_

"The interactive pie chart breaks down spending by category, while the detailed transaction table shows how MyAI has intelligently categorized each transaction. This transforms raw bank data into meaningful financial insights."

---

## 💰 **Savings Impact Calculator (20 seconds)**

"Here's where MyAI really shines - showing real financial impact. Our savings calculator demonstrates how much families can save by switching service providers like electricity companies."

_[Show SwitchingDemo component]_

"For example, switching electricity providers could save €720 annually - that's enough to fund a child's college savings plan. MyAI connects everyday financial decisions to long-term family goals."

---

## 🏠🚗 **Insurance Comparison Engine (35 seconds)**

"MyAI also includes comprehensive insurance comparison tools. Let's check home insurance quotes."

_[Navigate to Insurance → Home Insurance]_

"Users input their property details, and MyAI scrapes multiple insurance providers in real-time, presenting quotes in beautiful, sortable cards. We highlight the best value options and show potential savings."

_[Show getting quotes, results with comparison]_

"The same powerful comparison engine works for car insurance, helping families optimize their insurance costs across all major categories. Each quote includes detailed coverage information and savings calculations."

---

## 🎨 **Professional Design & Navigation (15 seconds)**

"Throughout the platform, you'll notice our professional financial app aesthetic - blues, grays, and whites creating trust and sophistication. The mobile-first responsive design works perfectly on all devices."

_[Show sidebar navigation, mobile responsiveness]_

"The sidebar menu includes our new 'Chat MyAI' option for easy navigation back to the main interface, with visual indicators showing your current location."

---

## 🎯 **Target Market & Value Proposition (15 seconds)**

"MyAI specifically targets affluent suburban families - households with significant financial complexity who need intelligent tools to optimize their money management, insurance costs, and long-term planning."

---

## 🚀 **Conclusion (10 seconds)**

"MyAI transforms financial management from overwhelming spreadsheets into intelligent, actionable insights. It's not just about tracking expenses - it's about empowering families to make smarter financial decisions that impact their future."

---

## 📝 **Key Features to Highlight During Demo:**

1. **AI-Powered Insights** - Real answers based on actual financial data
2. **Automated Categorization** - CSV upload → instant categorization
3. **Visual Analytics** - KPI cards, pie charts, traffic lights
4. **Insurance Comparison** - Real-time quotes from multiple providers
5. **Savings Calculator** - Connect daily decisions to long-term goals
6. **Professional UI/UX** - Trust-building design for financial services
7. **Mobile-First Design** - Works seamlessly across all devices
8. **Personalized Onboarding** - Tailored recommendations
9. **Secure Authentication** - Professional login experience
10. **Intelligent Navigation** - Intuitive user flow

---

## 🎬 **Recording Tips:**

- **Pace**: Speak clearly and confidently - 3 minutes goes by quickly
- **Flow**: Follow the navigation path: Login → Onboarding → Homepage → Upload → Insurance → Savings
- **Highlights**: Emphasize the AI intelligence and real-time processing
- **Value**: Connect features to real family financial benefits
- **Demo Data**: Use the built-in demo data for consistent results

---

## 📊 **Technical Architecture to Mention:**

- **Frontend**: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: FastAPI for transaction processing
- **AI Integration**: Custom chatbot with financial domain knowledge
- **APIs**: Real-time insurance scraping
- **Deployment**: GitHub Pages with automated CI/CD
